## 👤 About Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

**Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam** was born on **15 October 1931** in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

He studied physics and later aerospace engineering before beginning a career in India's space and defence research programs.

Dr. Kalam played an important role in India's space programme through his work with the **Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)**. He was closely associated with the development of satellite launch vehicle technology and contributed to the successful development of the **SLV-III**, which placed the Rohini satellite into orbit in 1980.

He later worked with the **Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO)** and became an important figure in India's guided missile development programme. His contributions earned him the popular title **"Missile Man of India."**

In **2002**, Dr. Kalam became the **11th President of India**. During his presidency, he became particularly popular among students and young people because of his emphasis on education, innovation, science, and national development.

After completing his presidential term, he continued teaching, writing, and interacting with students across India.

Dr. Kalam passed away on **27 July 2015** while delivering a lecture at the Indian Institute of Management Shillong.

His life continues to inspire millions of people, particularly students and young professionals, through his emphasis on knowledge, hard work, perseverance, and dreaming big.

## 🏆 Major Contributions

### 🚀 Indian Space Programme

Dr. Kalam worked with ISRO and contributed to India's early satellite launch vehicle programme.

He served as the project director of the **SLV-III**, India's first indigenous satellite launch vehicle, which successfully placed the **Rohini satellite into near-Earth orbit in 1980**.

---

### 🛰️ Missile Development

At DRDO, Dr. Kalam played a significant role in India's integrated guided missile development efforts.

His involvement in programmes associated with missiles such as **Agni** and **Prithvi** contributed to the development of India's indigenous defence capabilities.

His contributions to India's missile programme led to him being widely known as the:

> **"Missile Man of India"**

---

### 🇮🇳 Presidency of India

Dr. Kalam served as the **11th President of India from 2002 to 2007**.

During his presidency, he became especially popular among students and young people.

His approachable personality and focus on education made him one of India's most admired public figures.

---

### 🎓 Inspiration for Students

Education and youth development were central themes throughout Dr. Kalam's life.

He frequently interacted with students and encouraged them to develop knowledge, confidence, creativity, and a strong sense of responsibility toward society.

Even after his presidency, he continued travelling across India to teach and interact with students.

## 🏅 Awards and Honours

Dr. Kalam received several important awards and honours during his lifetime.

Some of the notable honours include:

- **Padma Bhushan** — 1981
- **Padma Vibhushan** — 1990
- **Bharat Ratna** — 1997
- **Hoover Medal** — 2009
- Honorary doctorates from several universities and institutions

The **Bharat Ratna**, India's highest civilian award, was awarded to him in recognition of his contribution to the country.

## 📚 Books by Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. Kalam was also a prolific author.

Some of his best-known books include:

### *Wings of Fire*

### *India 2020*

The book presents his vision for India as a developed nation and discusses science, technology, education, and national development.

### *Ignited Minds*

The book focuses on India's youth and encourages young people to develop their potential and contribute to the country's progress.

### *My Journey*

A collection of stories and experiences from different stages of his life.
